Dispersing Manifest Binary comes from mining digitized meteorites and residual data ore (which always drops 1~3).<br>

> Nearby Dispersing Manifest Binary attracts and merges automatically. Its name shows the contained Binary count; each entity holds at most 8 Binary units, and at 8 units its size is about twice that of a single Binary.
> ⚠ If you leave it alone for one minute, it will disappear.

It is obtained from the strange ore generated in the End. After accumulating over the years, it has become somewhat large (perhaps it has calcified?).<br>
