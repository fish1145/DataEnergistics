# ME Vacuum

<Row>
    <ItemImage id="data_energistics:me_vacuum" scale="4" />
    <RecipeFor id="data_energistics:me_vacuum" />
</Row>

The ME Vacuum is a ranged collection tool with internal storage slots. Collected contents are written directly into the installed ME storage cells instead of the player's inventory.

## How to use

Long press and hold the right mouse button to start extracting. If the crosshair hits a block within 5 blocks, the center is placed around that block; Otherwise, focus on a position about 2.5 blocks ahead of your line of sight to handle a 3×3×3 area.

Drops: Written directly into compatible item storage cells.
Fluids: A complete fluid source block is written into a compatible fluid storage cell. It is not collected unless the cell has room for the entire source block.
Dispersing Manifest Binary: Each entity adds one Data unit to a compatible Data Flow storage cell.
Other non-player entities: Pulled in front of the player. After holding onto the same entity for about 1.5 seconds, hold the right button while pressing the attack key to launch it at high speed.

Each successful absorption of a set of drops, a fluid source block, a Dispersing Manifest Binary, or a launch of an entity consumes 1,000 AE. The base energy capacity is 200,000 AE.

> Collection only succeeds when a compatible storage cell has enough free space and the vacuum has enough energy for one operation.
