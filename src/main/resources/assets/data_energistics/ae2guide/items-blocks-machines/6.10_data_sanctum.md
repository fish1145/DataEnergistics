# Data Sanctum

> Junior High School Quotes:  
> The destruction, constraint, and reconstruction of a galaxy is an exploration of the unknown.

> Placed here purely because it is essentially a single block

Data Sanctum is a large machine that automatically unfolds from a single object, capable of dissociating surrounding matter and producing Data Flow in black hole mode, and can also initiate two-way Portal to Meteorite Cluster dimensions. ⚠

> When placing it, a complete **5x5** floor area and an unobstructed space of **4 squares** above are required.

- The front display is used to view online status, switch operating modes, and show the black hole's range of effect.
- The network port on the back is used to connect ME cables. The main station will only go online after the port connects to a functioning ME network.
- Any part of the destruction structure will dismantle the entire terminal, and the machine body and internal items will be dropped uniformly by the main body.

## Work Mode

The front display of the terminal can switch between the following three modes:

### Standby

Stop black hole dissociation and dimensional teleportation, maintaining only networking and internal configuration.

### Black hole

Black holes gradually expand outward from the center of the terminal, with their maximum effective area centered around the terminal station and a radius of **40 blocks**. In a formal environment, processing is done every **10 seconds**, with a maximum of **20 blocks** dissociated per round.

- Each block dissected costs **2500 AE**.
- After successfully dissociating a block, each round produces **2000 Data Flow**.
- Ordinary entities swallowed by black holes each produce **2000 Data Flow**.
- Bedrock, AE2, and Data_Energistics cubes themselves will not be dissociated.
- Creator mode players, administrators, and players holding wireless terminals bound to the same ME network will not be devoured. ⚠

> Black holes directly remove dissociable blocks and entities within the range, without the usual block drops. Before activating, please confirm the scope of use, and if necessary, use the Range Display function on the display first.

The Data Flow generated first enters the return cache of the headquarters, then is automatically injected into the ME network connected to it. When the network lacks enough space to accommodate Data Flow, the dissociation process pauses.

### Portal

Portal mode opens the entrance to the **Meteorite Cluster** dimension at the terminal center. Players and other entities entering the central area of the main station are teleported, and a safe platform and return Portal are automatically generated in the target dimension.

Returning Portal will send the entity back to its original hub. When the terminal is offline, dismantled, or switched to another mode, the corresponding return channel will stop working.

## Energy Cache

The base energy cache at the main station is **500,000 AE** and has **3 energy card slots**. Each time an energy card is installed, the cache capacity doubles:

| Energy Card Count | Maximum cache |
| --- | ---: |
| 0 | 500,000 AE |
| 1 | 1,000,000 AE |
| 2 | 2,000,000 AE |
| 3 | 4,000,000 AE |

energy is automatically replenished from the connected ME network. Machine mode and black hole Range Display settings can also be saved and copied via ME memory.
