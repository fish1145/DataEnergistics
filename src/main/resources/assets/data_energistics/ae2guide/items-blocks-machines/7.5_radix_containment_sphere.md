# Radix Containment Sphere

<Row>
    <ItemImage id="data_energistics:radix_containment_sphere" scale="4" />
    <RecipeFor id="data_energistics:radix_containment_sphere" />
</Row>

The Radix Containment Sphere is a portable storage device dedicated to Binary. It uses 64 storage bytes at 8 Binary per byte and has its own AE energy reserve. A single resource type reserves one byte, leaving an effective capacity of 504 Binary.

---

## Capture Dispersing Manifest Binary

Hold a Radix Containment Sphere and right-click Dispersing Manifest Binary to capture it:

- Each capture consumes 1,000 AE.
- Each Dispersing Manifest Binary stores 1 Binary into the sphere.
- Binary capacity is fixed at 504 and will not be increased by upgrade cards.
- When the sphere's energy is depleted, it leaks one Manifest Binary into the world every 100 ticks.

## Binary Uses

- Right-click Deactivated Data Crystal Motherrock to directly convert it to Deficient Data Crystal Motherrock; This operation consumes 16 Binary and 150,000 AE.
- Other right-click recipes deduct the Binary and AE shown in their recipe details.

### Energy Card

<Row>
    <ItemLink id="ae2:energy_card" />
    <ItemImage id="ae2:energy_card" />
</Row>

Install up to 3 cards. Each energy card adds 100,000 AE capacity and increases charging speed:

| Number of energy cards | Maximum energy capacity |
|---:|---:|
| 0 | 50,000 AE |
| 1 | 150,000 AE |
| 2 | 250,000 AE |
| 3 | 350,000 AE |

> Maximum energy = 50,000 × (1 + 2 × energy card count)

### Fuzzy Card

<Row>
    <ItemLink id="ae2:fuzzy_card" />
    <ItemImage id="ae2:fuzzy_card" />
</Row>

Install up to one Fuzzy Card. Once installed, right-click a block with the Radix Containment Sphere to capture all Dispersing Manifest Binary within a 3-block radius of the clicked position, stopping when energy or storage space runs out.

> A Fuzzy Card occupies one upgrade slot. With one installed, at most two Energy Cards remain available, for a maximum capacity of 250,000 AE.
