# Resonance Crystals

Resonance crystal clusters do not grow from random ticks. Their conversion and growth are driven only by Warden sonic booms and by vibrations that are already travelling toward an eligible sculk sensor.

## Sonic-boom conversion

A Warden sonic boom checks the first tuning fork and the first crystal that can change along its extended ray independently. A full vanilla Amethyst Cluster or full Data Crystal Cluster becomes a Medium Resonance Crystal Cluster. Existing resonance stages advance from Small to Medium, Medium to Large, and Large to the mature Resonance Crystal Cluster. A mature cluster cannot change and does not prevent the ray from finding a later changeable crystal.

Facing and waterlogging are retained whenever a crystal is replaced. When a fork before the target successfully inserts Echo, it cancels the sonic boom's damage and knockback against that target; the sound, particles, and crystal conversion described here still resolve normally.

## Sensor-path interception

After vanilla vibration and wool-occlusion checks pass, the path toward each normal or calibrated sculk sensor is resolved independently in sound-travel order.

- A full Amethyst Cluster has a 25% chance to become a Small Resonance Crystal Cluster.
- A full Data Crystal Cluster has a 50% chance to become a Small Resonance Crystal Cluster.
- Small, Medium, and Large resonance stages always grow by one stage.
- Immature vanilla and data-crystal stages, and mature resonance clusters, do not intercept the sound.

A successful conversion or growth stops that vibration from reaching only the current sensor. A failed probability continues to later candidates. One sound that reaches several sensors can therefore affect the same crystal once per path.

## Active resonance wave

Every 1,000 loaded server ticks, a Resonance Crystal Block chooses one tuning fork uniformly from the loaded 7×7×7 area centred on the block. Ordinary blocks do not block the wave, but wool and other blocks that occlude vibration signals cancel that cycle without selecting another fork.

An unobstructed wave immediately applies the selected fork's normal 75% rule and then emits that fork variant's vibration event from the fork position, even if no Echo entered the network or the fork broke on the hit. The emitted event can be sensed or intercepted along other sensor paths.

Four Resonance Crystals craft into one Resonance Crystal Block, and the block unpacks into four crystals. Mature clusters use the vanilla Amethyst Cluster harvesting rules but drop Resonance Crystals; immature stages drop themselves only with Silk Touch.
