## Controls and storage cells

Sneak-use opens configuration. Each of the three modes accepts any AE-recognized storage cell, including item, fluid, data, and FE cells. The mode filters available ammunition, not the cell type. A cell without matching ammunition cannot fire. Removing a cell retains its contents.

Select a mode with its button and cycle ammunition with the ammo slot or AE's color-applicator wheel modifier: up selects next, down selects previous. Selection feedback appears above the hotbar. V selects rail, B selects crossbow; pressing the current mode key returns to grenade.

| Mode | Input | Projectile |
|---|---|---|
| Starfall Shard (grenade) | Hold attack to set range, release to fire | Follows the muzzle arc |
| Crystal Rift Crusher (rail) | Hold attack to charge, release one shot | Fires the ammunition displayed inside the barrel in a fast straight line |
| Entropy Thread Bow (crossbow) | Right-click loading and firing | Existing crossbow flight and upgrades |

## Rail ammunition

Full charge waits for release. Charging, cancellation, changing hands/cells, and switching mode cost nothing. Each valid release consumes a whole shot plus 200 AE. Partial charge still costs a whole shot and scales direct damage by the charge fraction. The chamber and projectile share the selected ammunition appearance. Damage happens at physical impact; FE briefly chains outward from that impact.

Ammunition sits directly in front of the cyan receiver opening. Data Flow and FE appear as plasma masses with surface lightning; their cyan and green hues are sampled from their respective textures. Chamber and flight visuals are identical.

Firing brakes the rails through half their stroke in 0.1 seconds and returns them over 0.6 seconds, with translucent exhaust from both sides. The full normal cycle takes 0.7 seconds (14 ticks). Blaze Rod, Data Flow and FE can begin another charge as soon as braking finishes. During return, the charge cap rises with recovered distance; shot strength is the lower of charge progress and the recovered fraction. Repeat shots brake from the current rail position and still consume a whole round and 200 AE each. Heavy Core does not support early charging: its 0.3-second brake and 1.8-second return require the full 2.1-second (42-tick) cooldown. Switching ammo cannot shorten a fired heavy round's cooldown, and selecting Heavy Core during return still requires full recovery before charging.

Values below are at full charge. The second focusing card retains first-card improvements unless replaced.

| Ammo | 0 cards | 1 card | 2 cards | Resource per shot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blaze Rod | 8 damage; burn 5s at 1.5/s | 10 damage; burn 10s at 1.5/s | 14 damage; burn 10s at 3/s | 1 item |
| Heavy Core | 34 damage; Slowness IV | 40 damage; Slowness IV | 40 damage; Slowness X; additional 25% missing-health damage | 1 item |
| Data Flow | 13 damage; one 4s Radix Loss stack | 13 damage; two 9s stacks | 20 damage; two 9s stacks; stacking bonus coefficient becomes 15% of maximum health | 1500 Data Flow |
| FE | 11 damage to living entities in a 4x4 impact area | 11 damage in 7x7 | 19 damage in 7x7 | 800 FE |

FE excludes its shooter; area numbers denote full widths. Heavy Core Slowness lasts one second; missing health is sampled before impact damage. The two-card Data Flow bonus uses existing stacks multiplied by 15% of target maximum health and retains the usual maximum-stack burst. Charge also scales percentage bonus damage, but not burn values or effect durations.

## Grenade ammunition

| Ammo | 0 cards | 1 card | 2 cards |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wind Charge | 5x5, 7 damage, 9-block lift | 7x7, 13 damage, 9-block lift | 7x7, 26 damage, 28-block lift |
| Fire Charge | 5x5, 4 direct damage, burn 5s at 1.5/s | 7x7, 6 direct damage, burn 5s at 1.5/s | 7x7, 6 direct damage, burn 14s at 3/s |

Wind affects living entities without moving terrain. Fire ignites valid ground surfaces. Burn refreshes preserve damage cadence and respect extinguishing and fire resistance.

TNT, AE2 Tiny TNT, Configurable TNT, Digital Annihilator, and supported mod TNT retain native impact detonation. Draconic Evolution reactor cores retain their native explosion process. Each grenade costs one item and 200 AE.

## Crossbow and upgrades

Colored Matter Balls retain their damage and apply matching outline colors for five seconds. Singularity pulls a 3x3/5x5/5x5 area at 0/1/2 cards; two cards detonate after 10 ticks for 15% of target maximum health.

Singularity Cube has 36/40/40 base damage and 4/6/8 fragments, each dealing one quarter of parent base damage. Redstone tuning enables fragment homing; fragments do not split again. Data Residual Crystal inherits saber projectile behavior and is consumed without recovery. Its maximum-health ratio caps at 10%, starting at 200000 AE, gaining one percentage point per additional 25000 AE, up to 425000 AE.

Energy cards expand the 200000 AE base capacity. Speed cards improve projectile speed; four speed cards still enable crossbow auto-fire after loading. Rail mode does not shorten cooldown or auto-repeat through speed cards. Redstone homing remains a crossbow feature and multiplies ordinary crossbow shot energy by five.
