Durability is 20 kae, allowing various Matter Balls, singularities, and fully charged Data Light Saber to be upgraded as ammunition.

## Firing modes

The weapon itself is no longer an AE storage cell. Sneak-use opens the AE configuration screen, where each mode has a removable item cell. The Cell column contains the mode's disk, the Ammo column shows its selected compatible ammunition, and the lower slots are the player inventory. Click an arrow to select the mode and its cell, then click its ammunition slot to cycle types (right-click reverses the direction); scrolling over the active ammo slot also cycles types. While holding the weapon, use the same mouse-wheel modifier as AE's color applicator (left Shift by default): scroll up for the next compatible type and down for the previous one.

Changing mode or ammunition briefly displays the current selection above the hotbar. Changes made in the configuration screen are shown after closing it. The weapon name and screen title still display only the current mode name.

Shots consume ammunition from the active mode's cell, retaining the cell's capacity, remaining contents, item components, and partition rules. Removing a cell takes its contents with it; a new weapon without a cell cannot load ammunition. Legacy ammunition stored directly on the weapon is migrated into a real 1k item cell without overwriting installed cells.

| Mode | Input | Projectile |
|---|---|---|
| Digitized-Starfall Shard (grenade) | Hold attack (left mouse by default) to set range; release to fire | Gravity and drag follow the arc preview from the muzzle |
| Digitized-Crystal Rift Crusher (rail) | Hold attack to charge; release to fire | No gravity; damage equals full-charge damage multiplied by the charge fraction |
| Digitized-Entropy Thread Bow (crossbow) | Keep the original right-click loading and firing sequence | Existing crossbow ammo and upgrade behavior |

V selects rail mode and B selects crossbow mode. Pressing the selected mode's key again returns to grenade mode. Cannons keep charging until release, including with four speed cards. Switching mode, changing held items, or opening a menu cancels charging.

Grenade launch speed interpolates from 0.65 to the ammunition's nominal speed. Rail launch speed is `max(8, nominal speed * 2)`. Speed cards still increase nominal speed. Cannon shots disable homing to preserve their flight path and do not charge its additional regular-ammo energy cost.

Grenade mode also accepts TNT blocks, including vanilla TNT, AE2 Tiny TNT, Configurable TNT, the Digital Annihilator, and other modded TNT variants. TNT follows the previewed arc and immediately triggers its own explosion or activation on hitting the ground, a wall, or an entity. Its native power, special effects, and water behavior are preserved. Stored TNT remains in the weapon when switching to rail or crossbow mode, but can only be fired after returning to grenade mode.

With Draconic Evolution installed, grenade mode also accepts the reactor core (`draconicevolution:reactor_core`). The projectile displays the core's own model and starts the native full-fuel reactor explosion on hitting the ground, a wall, or an entity: a base radius of 350 blocks multiplied by Draconic Evolution's explosion scale. The native process calculates terrain in stages before applying destruction, damage, and effects, without an additional fuse countdown; charging only changes the firing range. If large reactor explosions are disabled in Draconic Evolution, the core instead uses its small explosion and ejected-lava behavior.

## Ammunition rules

Grenade mode adds Wind Charges and Fire Charges. Wind Charges use 5x5 or 7x7 living-entity areas, launch targets 9 or 28 blocks upward, and deal 7, 13, or 26 damage by focusing-card count. Fire Charges use a 5x5 or 7x7 area, deal 4 or 6 direct damage, and apply 5 or 14 seconds of burn at 1.5 or 3 damage per second. Wind Charges do not break terrain; Fire Charges light valid ground positions.

Rail mode is a continuous ray. Blaze Rod deals 8/10/14 damage per tick, Heavy Core deals 16/25/25 damage per tick and applies Slowness, Data Flow deals 3/3/6 damage per tick and applies Radix Loss at the card-selected interval, and FE chains to living entities in the aim-point area at a 5/3/1.5 game-tick interval. Regular shots last 10 seconds, Heavy Core lasts 13.5 seconds, and every completed or interrupted shot enters a 4-second cooldown. Data Flow, FE, and AE consumption are settled from the actual firing duration.

Crossbow Singularity pulls living entities inward; its second focusing card detonates after the pull and deals 15% of each target's maximum health. Singularity Cubes split into 4/6/8 fragments at 0/1/2 cards, with 36/40/40 parent damage and one quarter damage per fragment. Data Residual Crystals inherit Data Light Saber projectile behavior, cap maximum-health damage at 10%, and are consumed on hit without recovery.
