# Data Capture Ball

<Row>
    <ItemImage id="data_energistics:data_capture_ball" scale="4" />
    <RecipeFor id="data_energistics:data_capture_ball" />
</Row>

The Data Capture Ball is a portable storage device dedicated to Data. It uses 16 storage bytes at 8 Data per byte and has its own AE energy reserve. Its single resource-type reservation leaves an effective capacity of 120 Data.

---

## Capture Dispersing Manifest Binary

Hold a Data Capture Ball and right-click Dispersing Manifest Binary to capture it:

- Each capture consumes 1,000 AE.
- Each Dispersing Manifest Binary stores 1 data point into the ball.
- Data capacity is fixed at 120 and will not be increased by upgrade cards.
- When energy is depleted to 0 AE, Data Capture Ball is immediately destroyed, and all stored data is lost.

> A newly produced ball contains only 1,000 AE. Capturing once would reduce it to zero and destroy it, so charge it before use.

## Data Uses

- Crafting a Data Reassembler requires at least 8 Data in the ball. The ball is returned after crafting with 8 Data consumed.
- Right-click Deactivated Data Crystal Motherrock to directly convert it to Deficient Data Crystal Motherrock; This operation consumes 16 data points and 150,000 AE.
- Other right-click recipes deduct the Data and AE shown in their recipe details.

### Energy Card

<Row>
    <ItemLink id="ae2:energy_card" />
    <ItemImage id="ae2:energy_card" />
</Row>

Install up to 3 cards. Each energy card adds 100,000 AE capacity and increases charging speed:

| Number of energy cards | Maximum energy capacity |
|---:|---:|
| 0 | 50,000 AE |
| 1 | 150,000 AE |
| 2 | 250,000 AE |
| 3 | 350,000 AE |

> Maximum energy = 50,000 × (1 + 2 × energy card count)

### Fuzzy Card

<Row>
    <ItemLink id="ae2:fuzzy_card" />
    <ItemImage id="ae2:fuzzy_card" />
</Row>

Install up to one Fuzzy Card. Once installed, right-click a block with the Data Capture Ball to capture all Dispersing Manifest Binary within a 3-block radius of the clicked position, stopping when energy or storage space runs out.

> A Fuzzy Card occupies one upgrade slot. With one installed, at most two Energy Cards remain available, for a maximum capacity of 250,000 AE.
