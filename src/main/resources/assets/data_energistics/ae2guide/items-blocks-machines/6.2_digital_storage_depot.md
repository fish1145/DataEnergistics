# Digital Storage Depot

>Digital Storage Depot is a hybrid storage block that can access ME networks. It can simultaneously store items, fluids, as well as non-material Wrapped Stacks such as Data Flow and data.

## Storage Structure

> storage compartment, there are 21 item slots, 3 fluid slots, and 3 Wrapped Stacks slots.

- The item slot can store ordinary items and is accessed via the network as ME Storage
- The fluid tank has a base capacity of 64,000 mB per cell
- The Wrapped Stacks slot has a base capacity of 64,000 units per compartment, suitable for storing Data Flow, data, and other non-material, non-fluid Wrapped Stacks
- The same fluid or Wrapped Stacks occupies only one slot and cannot be recorded multiple times in multiple slots

Inserting an AE2 capacity card expands all three types of capacity. ⚠

<Row>
  <ItemLink id="ae2:capacity_card" />
  <ItemImage id="ae2:capacity_card" />
</Row>

> Capacity formula:  
> Final capacity = base capacity × (1 + 4 × capacity card count)  
> By default, up to 4 capacity cards can be installed. When removing the capacity card, if the existing content exceeds the downgraded capacity, the card will be protected and cannot be removed.

## ME Network

>Digital Storage Depot after connecting to the ME network, internal content is mounted as a separate storage unit.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="ae2:fluix_covered_cable" />
  <ItemImage id="data_energistics:digital_storage_depot" />
</Row>

It supports AE2 storage priority. The higher the priority, the more the network tends to write content here first; The lower the priority, the better it is for overflow or backup storage.

AE2 export bus can also be used with storage warehouses. After configuring Wrapped Stacks such as Data Flow and data to the export bus, they can be directly imported into the storage warehouse's Wrapped Stacks slot; For ordinary items and fluids, they are handled according to the corresponding slots.

---

## Auto Export

There are three Status:  Auto Export buttons in the left toolbar  
- Off: Do not proactively output internal content
- Container: Automatically pushes items, fluids, and Wrapped Stacks to adjacent containers
- AE: Automatically pushes items, fluids, and Wrapped Stacks back to the currently connected AE network

When the Auto Export is in "container" mode, you can open Output Side configuration. Items, fluids, and Wrapped Stacks each have independent six-sided switches, allowing different types of content to reach different devices.

---

## Portable Barrel Mode ⚠

> When held, Digital Storage Depot can also be used as a portable container. By default, press 'Z' to switch to barrel mode.

After enabling barrel mode:

- Right-click Fluid Source or Fluid Container, which can store a bucket of fluid into the currently selected fluid tank
- Right-click on a placement position to pour a bucket of fluid from the current fluid tank
- Only one marked fluid tank will be exposed as an item fluid container to other devices
- If the slot is marked as Wrapped Stacks, it will participate in AE interaction as the corresponding Wrapped Stacks type portable container

Select Slot:

- Hold down the Ctrl wheel to switch the current fluid slot
- Hold down the Alt wheel to switch the current Wrapped Stacks slot ⚠

> The prompt box previews existing items, fluids, and Wrapped Stacks in the storage bin. When replaced, the stored fluid and Wrapped Stacks are restored along with the block data.
