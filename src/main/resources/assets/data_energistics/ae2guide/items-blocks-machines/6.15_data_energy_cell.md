# Data Energy Cell

The Data Energy Cell is a high-capacity energy buffer that can connect directly to an AE network. It can absorb AE from the network and provide AE to it, making it suitable as a large-network buffer, a priority power node, or a local power supply for machines.

The recipe is available in the Data Integrated Charger's Inscribing mode.

## Energy Parameters

Maximum storage  **32,000,000 AE**<br>
Network charge rate  **16,000 AE/t**<br>
Network priority  **16**<br>

The cell's energy is stored in its block entity. Stored AE is preserved when the block is broken and placed again; when a charged cell is carried as an item, its item component also preserves the current energy.
The Data Energy Cell only provides an AE network energy-storage interface and does not automatically convert AE to FE.

## Charge State

The cell switches between five visual states according to its stored-energy ratio: empty, low, medium, high, and full. You can view its current and maximum storage with an empty hand or an information overlay such as Jade.
The cell also provides a redstone comparator signal:
- An entirely empty cell outputs 0;
- Once it stores any AE, it outputs at least 1;
- The closer its storage is to the limit, the closer the comparator signal is to 15.

This allows a comparator to monitor the energy buffer and switch network-control logic when the energy is nearly depleted or nearly full.
