# Darkstring Data Settlement Tool

> Junior High School Quotes:
A crossbow that fires condensed matter, a symphony of power and beauty, tracing the most violent arcs of civilization in the air.

Durability is 20 kae, allowing various Matter Balls, singularities, and fully charged Data Light Saber to be upgraded as ammunition.

## Firing modes

| Mode | Input | Projectile |
|---|---|---|
| Digitized-Starfall Shard (grenade) | Hold attack (left mouse by default) to set range; release to fire | Gravity and drag follow the arc preview from the muzzle |
| Digitized-Crystal Rift Crusher (rail) | Hold attack to charge; release to fire | No gravity; damage equals full-charge damage multiplied by the charge fraction |
| Digitized-Entropy Thread Bow (crossbow) | Keep the original right-click loading and firing sequence | Existing crossbow ammo and upgrade behavior |

V selects rail mode and B selects crossbow mode. Pressing the selected mode's key again returns to grenade mode. Cannons keep charging until release, including with four speed cards. Switching mode, changing held items, or opening a menu cancels charging.

Grenade launch speed interpolates from 0.65 to the ammunition's nominal speed. Rail launch speed is `max(8, nominal speed * 2)`. Speed cards still increase nominal speed. Cannon shots disable homing to preserve their flight path and do not charge its additional regular-ammo energy cost.

Grenade mode also accepts TNT blocks, including vanilla TNT, AE2 Tiny TNT, Configurable TNT, the Digital Annihilator, and other modded TNT variants. TNT follows the previewed arc and immediately triggers its own explosion or activation on hitting the ground, a wall, or an entity. Its native power, special effects, and water behavior are preserved. Stored TNT remains in the weapon when switching to rail or crossbow mode, but can only be fired after returning to grenade mode.
